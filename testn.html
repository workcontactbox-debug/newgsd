Frequently Asked Questions

Structural Steel Detailing FAQ

Quick answers about structural steel detailing, BIM modeling, fabrication drawings, erection documentation and machine-ready data.

Structural steel detailing is the process of converting structural engineering information into detailed documentation used for steel fabrication, assembly and erection. It includes member information, dimensions, connections, materials, piece marks and related fabrication references.

Steel BIM modeling can include primary and secondary framing, structural members, connections, assemblies, miscellaneous steel and coordinated model information.

Shop drawings provide fabricators with detailed information needed to manufacture steel members and assemblies accurately, including dimensions, materials, piece marks, holes, bolts and weld information.

Erection and General Arrangement drawings communicate the overall arrangement and positioning of fabricated steel components through plans, elevations, sections, grids, levels and erection references.

Yes. Anchor bolt and base plate documentation can communicate plate dimensions, anchor bolt locations, orientation, offsets, hole patterns and relevant foundation interface information.

Machine-readable fabrication files can be prepared as required by the project's detailing and fabrication workflow.

The workflow connects design understanding, 3D modeling, connection development, shop drawing production, erection documentation and fabrication data so information remains consistent through each stage.

Coordination helps maintain consistency between structural information, the steel model, fabrication documentation and field erection requirements, reducing avoidable conflicts and interpretation issues.
