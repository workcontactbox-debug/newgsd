Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about steel detailing & BIM.

Structural steel detailing is the process of converting structural design information into detailed models and drawings used for steel fabrication and erection. It can include member detailing, connections, shop drawings, erection documentation and fabrication information.
Structural steel BIM modelling is the development of a detailed 3D digital model representing the steel structure and its components. It can support visualization, coordination, detailing, documentation and fabrication workflows.
Shop drawings provide fabrication teams with detailed information about steel members, dimensions, connections, materials, bolts, welds, assemblies and other requirements needed to manufacture the structure.
Steel BIM coordination involves reviewing the structural steel model with other building disciplines to identify clashes, clearance problems and coordination issues before fabrication and installation.
Connection detailing can include the detailed representation of plates, bolts, welds, stiffeners, gussets, base plates and other components required to communicate how structural steel members connect.
A coordinated BIM workflow can help identify clashes and inconsistencies earlier and can improve consistency between the structural model and associated documentation. BIM is most effective when combined with experienced detailing, project standards, quality control and coordination.
